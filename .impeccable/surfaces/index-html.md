# Home — foundstudio.cl

Scope: the single page index.html. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: pyme owners in Huechuraba/Santiago, on their phones, arriving from WhatsApp/Instagram. Action: send the form (quote) or WhatsApp.
Proof: interactive fictional examples by trade (labeled "Ejemplo ficticio") + real portfolio cases (placeholders until assets arrive). No invented prices, metrics or testimonials.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page IS the before and after: a single browser window (the one from the logo) pinned on screen that starts as "ERROR 404 · tu negocio no aparece" and, with the scroll, gets fixed into real pages for each trade. It refuses the generic agency hero (headline + subcopy + services grid).
OWN-WORLD: Navy #1E2A4A ground, cream #F2ECDF ink and chrome; window with a thick cream frame, three dots, mono URL pill and a hard navy-deep shadow (taken from the logo icon). Fredoka for display (the bubbly letters of the logo), Manrope for text, JetBrains Mono only in the URL bar and step labels. Each trade brings its own world inside the window; outside, only navy and cream.
STORY: I see that my business doesn't show up → I see five businesses like mine come alive (I book an hour, I add to the cart) → "¿Y tu negocio?" I choose my trade → form with my trade preselected, or WhatsApp.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left column with the header "¿Tu negocio da 404?" in Fredoka 44–64px and the line "Te hacemos {found}.", primary button "Quiero mi página" and a scroll hint; right, the window at ~60% width with the 404 glitching and the URL "tunegocio.cl". Mobile: short caption at the top, window filling the rest of the viewport. Primary action in the header + caption.
FORM: Pinned prototype (scroll-scrubbed browser-window story), translated to GSAP ScrollTrigger: one pin, clip-path mask wipe between trades, URL typed letter by letter, inner page scrolled with the scrub, glow color per chapter. Seed key: none — direction pinned by the brief (prototipo aprobado), concept-seed not run.
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: the window wipe + typed URL; inside, booking an hour (barbería) and the cart (almacén) work for real.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
